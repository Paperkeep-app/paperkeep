Download your Notion invoice: the exact path

Notion doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Go to Settings in your sidebar.
  2. Select Billing.
  3. Under Invoices, select View invoice.

Good to know: Notion says it does not email invoices at this time.

Source: Notion Help Center.

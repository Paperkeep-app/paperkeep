Download your Hostinger invoice as a PDF: the exact path

Hostinger invoices sit in the Billing section of your dashboard, payment by payment. For your company details to appear on them, you must enter those details before the purchase. Here is the path, according to Hostinger's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In the Hostinger dashboard, go to Billing › Payment History.
  2. Click the arrow (❯) next to the relevant payment.
  3. In the Payment Details window, click Download Invoice.

Good to know: “The invoice will be downloaded as a PDF file to the default download location set in your browser.”

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an invoice with my VAT number?

According to Hostinger's help, in the EU “tax-compliant invoices are issued only for entities with a valid tax identification number”. Add your company name and VAT number to your account before your next purchase, or tick Add company details at checkout.

Can I correct an invoice that has already been issued?

No: “Issued invoices cannot be changed or customized.” New details only appear on future invoices.

I can't find the arrow next to the payment.

Make sure you are in Payment History, under Billing.

Source: Hostinger official help, setting up your tax code, read on 27/10/2026.

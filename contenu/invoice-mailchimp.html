Mailchimp invoice: where to download your receipt (and title it “Invoice”)

Mailchimp automatically e-mails a receipt to your account's billing contact. It is titled “receipt”, but an option lets you title it “Invoice” for your accounts. Here is where to find and download these documents, according to Mailchimp's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Click your profile icon and choose Account.
  2. Click the Billing drop-down and choose Billing history.
  3. Use the Date drop-down to filter your receipts if needed.
  4. Use the drop-down for the receipt and select Download (or Print, or Email Invoice).

Good to know: for several receipts, tick their checkboxes, then click Download selected.

Source: Mailchimp official help, understanding your receipt, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

My accountant wants an invoice, not a receipt.

In Account & billing › Billing › Billing information, Notifications section, choose Label receipts as invoices: “Choose this option to label your receipt header as 'Invoice' for legal or accounting reasons.” Only the title changes.

How do I add my VAT number?

In Account & billing › Billing › Billing information, edit the primary account contact and fill in the VAT or tax ID field. It shows on future receipts, not on past ones.

I don't get the receipt by e-mail.

It goes automatically to the billing contact listed in your account: check that address in your billing information.

Download your Dropbox invoice: the exact path

Dropbox doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Log in to dropbox.com and click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
  2. Click Manage Account, then Billing.
  3. Next to the transaction, click Invoice or Receipt.

Good to know: a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store does not appear on dropbox.com.

Source: Dropbox Help.

Download your OVHcloud invoice: the exact path

OVHcloud doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In the OVHcloud Control Panel, click your name in the top right.
  2. Select My bills.
  3. On the invoice's row, click …, then See PDF version.

Good to know: OVHcloud also emails each invoice to you as a link.

Source: OVHcloud documentation.

Claude (Anthropic) invoice: where to download it (exact path)

Anthropic e-mails the invoice for your Claude subscription after each charge, with the subject “Your receipt from Anthropic”. Lost it, or need your VAT number on it? It also waits in Claude's settings. Here is the path, according to Claude's official help.

The path, step by step

Pro or Max plan

  1. Go to Settings › Billing.
  2. Find the Invoices section.
  3. Click View next to the invoice you want to open.

Team plan (organization owner)

  1. Go to Organization settings › Billing.
  2. In the Invoices section, click View: a new Stripe tab opens.
  3. Click Download invoice.

Good to know: “If you subscribed through Claude for iOS or Claude for Android, Anthropic doesn't issue your invoices”: the receipt then comes from Apple or Google.

Source: Claude official help, Pro and Max plan invoices and Team plan billing FAQs, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my company name and VAT number on the invoice?

On Pro or Max, in Settings › Billing, tick “Use a different name on invoices” when you add or update your payment method; the help also says you can add a tax or VAT ID. On Team, use the billing settings and the “Update” button.

Can I correct an invoice that has already been issued?

No. According to Claude's help, “Changes to your billing details apply to future invoices only. Once an invoice has been issued, we can't edit or reissue it.”

I can't find the invoice e-mail.

Search for the subject “Your receipt from Anthropic” in your billing e-mail inbox, or go to Settings › Billing.

Apple invoice: where to find the receipt for a purchase or subscription (iCloud+, App Store)

Apple e-mails a receipt for your purchases and subscriptions (iCloud+, apps, Apple Music…), and may combine several on one invoice. Can't find it any more? It stays in your Apple Account's purchase history. Here is where to find it, according to Apple's official help.

The path, step by step

On the web (simplest on a computer)

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with your Apple Account.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. In the App Store, tap your photo or initials.
  2. Tap Apps & Purchase History, then Purchase History.
  3. To go further back, tap Last 90 days and pick another filter.

Good to know: the history includes “a list of apps, subscriptions, music, and other purchased content.” In your mailbox, search for “receipt from Apple” or “invoice from Apple”.

Source: Apple official help, see your purchase history and find a receipt, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

Why one receipt for several purchases?

Apple's help explains that Apple may combine several purchases and subscriptions on one invoice.

I paid a subscription (ChatGPT, Claude, Canva…) on my iPhone. Where is the invoice?

At Apple: when the subscription goes through the App Store, Apple takes the payment and the receipt is in your Apple purchase history, not with the publisher.

I can't find the receipt e-mail.

Apple's help suggests searching your e-mail for “receipt from Apple” or “invoice from Apple”, or going to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Download your Adobe invoice (Creative Cloud, Acrobat): the exact path

Adobe doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Sign in to your Adobe account.
  2. In the Orders and invoices window, select View invoices for your plan or product.
  3. In Invoice and billing history, select the Download PDF icon.

Good to know: Adobe says it does not send monthly automated email invoices. Teams customers use the Admin Console.

Source: Adobe Help.

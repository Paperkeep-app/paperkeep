Download your Wix invoice: the exact path

The invoices for your Wix subscriptions and services are in your account's Billing History, each one as a PDF. Here is the path, according to Wix's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In your Wix account, go to Billing History.
  2. Next to the subscription or service, click the More Actions icon.
  3. Click View Invoice.
  4. Click the Download icon (or the print icon).

Good to know: “Invoices are downloaded in PDF format.” The history can also be exported as CSV.

Source: Wix official help, downloading invoices, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the name or address on the invoice?

In Billing History: More Actions › Update invoice details, then Save. In the European Union, the VAT ID and country cannot be changed there.

Can I correct a past invoice?

No: according to Wix's help, invoice details cannot be updated for past invoices.

Where do I enter my VAT number?

At checkout, in the VAT ID field. Wix's help says: “The VAT information you enter applies to future invoices only (including your current purchase).”

Source: Wix official help, updating your invoices and VAT in Europe, read on 27/10/2026.

Download your Meta Ads invoice (Facebook and Instagram ads): the exact path

Meta Ads doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Go to Billing and payments.
  2. If you have access to several ad accounts, select the right one in Accounts.
  3. Click Payment activity in the left menu.
  4. Choose the date range, then, on the transaction's row, click Download below Action.

Good to know: this feature is only available on desktop. Meta calls this document a receipt.

Source: Meta Business Help Centre.

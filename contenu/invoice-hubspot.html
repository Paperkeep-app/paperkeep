HubSpot invoice: where to view and download it as a PDF (exact path)

The invoices for your HubSpot subscription sit in Account & Billing, on the Transactions tab, next to your payment receipts. Here is the path, according to HubSpot's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In your HubSpot account, click your account name in the top right corner, then Account & Billing.
  2. Click the Transactions tab: the most recent are at the top, and the filters in the top right separate refunds, payments, invoices and orders.
  3. Click View under an invoice to view it, or Download to download a PDF.

Good to know: a payment receipt is on the same tab: in the top right, click Payments and select Processed.

Source: HubSpot official help, view, download and pay your HubSpot invoice, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

Do I get the invoice by e-mail?

Only in some cases: “If your contract is set up on invoicing terms, an email with your invoice information will also be sent.” Otherwise, use the Transactions tab.

How do I get my VAT number on the invoice?

In Account & Billing, Company Info tab, enter your Tax ID in the Company Information section. Only users with Account Access permissions can add or update it.

Can I correct an invoice that has already been paid?

No: “It is not possible to update or change company details on invoices that have already been paid.”

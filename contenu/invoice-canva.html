Download your Canva invoice: the exact path

Canva doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. On the homepage, select your account profile to open the menu.
  2. Choose Settings, then the Orders and invoices tab.
  3. Find your purchase and select View invoice: the invoice downloads as a PDF.

Good to know: for a subscription paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play, the receipt is with Apple or Google.

Source: Canva Help Center.

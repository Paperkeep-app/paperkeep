Download your Figma invoice as a PDF: the exact path

Figma invoices sit in the admin area of your team or organization, and only admins can see them. Here is the path, according to Figma's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Log in at figma.com to open the file browser.
  2. Click Admin.
  3. Select the Billing tab, then the Invoices tab.
  4. Select an invoice to open the invoice details panel.
  5. Download a PDF of the invoice from that panel.

Good to know: this applies to Professional, Organization and Enterprise plans, for admins only. The invoice number is clickable and opens the invoice in Stripe.

Source: Figma official help, manage payment and invoice details, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my company name and address on the invoice?

In Admin, Settings tab, under Billing: Invoice details. Enter the name and address, then click Save.

Where do I add my VAT number?

In Admin › Settings, under Billing: Value Added Tax / Goods and Services Tax. The option only exists in countries where Figma charges VAT or GST.

I can't see the Invoices tab.

According to Figma's help, it is for admins on paid plans only. Ask one of your team's admins.

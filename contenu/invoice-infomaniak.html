Download your Infomaniak invoice as a PDF: the exact path

Infomaniak keeps its invoices in the Manager, on the billing documents page. Final invoices can only be printed once they are paid, and the PDF is only attached to renewal e-mails if you switch it on. Here is the path, according to Infomaniak's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Open the invoice history in the Infomaniak Manager.
  2. If needed, filter by status (such as Paid) and by period.
  3. Click the invoice in the table.
  4. Click the Download button, or the chevron next to it for more options.

Good to know: you need at least the right to manage accounting. For several invoices at once, filter first, then pick the export type at the bottom (CSV file or e-mail delivery).

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I print my invoice?

According to Infomaniak's help, final invoices can only be printed after they have been paid. Also check that your user has the right to manage accounting.

How do I get the PDF by e-mail?

In the Manager's accounting configuration, switch on PDF invoice attachments. Changes are saved automatically.

Is a pro forma invoice enough for my accounts?

No. Infomaniak's help states that pro forma invoices have no legal, tax or accounting value: keep the final invoice.

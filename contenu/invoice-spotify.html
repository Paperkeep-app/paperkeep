Spotify invoice: where to download your Premium receipt

Spotify calls it a “receipt”, not an invoice. It is only e-mailed when you start a trial or make the first payment of a new subscription; the others wait in your account, for the last 2 years only. Here is the path, according to Spotify's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In your Spotify account, go to Payment history.
  2. Under Your payments, select the payment you want to view.
  3. Select Manage, then View receipts.
  4. Choose Download at the bottom of the page.

Good to know: “Receipts are available for transactions from the last 2 years.” Collect them as you go.

Source: Spotify official help, view receipts, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

I don't get a receipt by e-mail every month. Is that normal?

Yes. According to Spotify's help, you get an e-mail receipt when you start a trial or make your first payment of a new subscription; the others are in your payment history.

I need a receipt older than 2 years.

Spotify's help says receipts are available for transactions from the last 2 years. For older ones, contact Spotify.

My payment isn't in the history.

Spotify has a separate help article for subscriptions billed through Google Play. If you pay through a third party, look for the receipt there.

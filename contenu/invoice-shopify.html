Shopify invoice: where to download your subscription bill as a PDF

This is about the bill Shopify sends you for your plan and fees (not the invoices your store sends to customers). It sits in the admin's Billing section and can be exported as a PDF. Here is the path, according to Shopify's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Settings › Billing.
  2. In the Past bills section, click the bill you want to export.
  3. Click Export bill.
  4. In the dialog, select PDF.
  5. Click Export bill again.

Good to know: for your whole history, use Past bills › menu icon › Export bills; that file is a CSV sent to your account's e-mail address, not a PDF.

Source: Shopify official help, exporting your bills, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my VAT number?

In Settings › Billing, click Billing profile, then Add VAT number in the VAT number section. Shopify checks it with the EU's VIES system.

I changed my address: are my old bills updated?

No. Shopify's help says that “only new invoices issued after the billing profile was updated will reflect the new information.”

How far back does the history go?

According to the help, you can export billing data over any time range, back to the date you created your Shopify account.

Source: Shopify official help, billing taxes in the EU, read on 27/10/2026.

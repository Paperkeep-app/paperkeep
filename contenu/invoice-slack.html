Slack invoice: where to find your billing statement (exact path)

Slack calls its invoice a “billing statement”. Each month has one, to view and download from the workspace's admin tools. Here is the path, according to Slack's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. In the sidebar, click Admin.
  2. Select Manage billing.
  3. Open the History tab.
  4. Click the calendar icon, select a month, then click Go To to view the statement.

Good to know: only Workspace Owners and members who upgraded the workspace manage billing. Billing contacts can see some information, including billing statements, but cannot change billing details.

Source: Slack official help, manage your Slack plan and billing details, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my VAT number?

In Manage billing, open Billing Details, click Change, then Save Billing Address. The help says you can add your VAT/ABN ID and business status there.

Can I correct a statement that has already been issued?

Not if you pay by credit card: “It isn't possible to edit billing statements that have already been issued if you pay for Slack with a credit card.”

I can't see the History tab.

Check your role: billing is reserved for Workspace Owners and the members who upgraded the workspace.

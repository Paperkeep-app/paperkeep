Download your Microsoft 365 invoice (business): the exact path

Microsoft 365 doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Go to the Microsoft 365 admin center.
  2. In the dashboard view, go to Billing › Bills & payments; in the simplified view, select Products, then View invoices.
  3. Select the invoice in the list, then Download and Download invoice.

Good to know: you must be at least a Billing Administrator.

Source: Microsoft documentation.

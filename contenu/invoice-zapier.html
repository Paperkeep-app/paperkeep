Zapier invoice: where to find it and save it as a PDF (exact path)

Zapier shows its invoices in your account's billing settings but has no PDF button: you go through your browser's print function. You can also have each paid invoice e-mailed. Here is the path, according to Zapier's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Open the Billing and usage settings.
  2. Select the Billing settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the Invoices section.
  4. Click View Details next to the invoice: it opens in a new window or tab.
  5. Right-click the invoice, choose Print, then Save as PDF.

Good to know: invoices are tied to the account you are viewing. If you have several, switch accounts from the menu in the top right corner.

Source: Zapier official help, access and manage your invoices, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get invoices by e-mail?

In the Billing settings tab, Invoice Information section, click Update Info and tick the option to e-mail a copy of paid invoices. Without another address, they go to your account e-mail.

How do I add my VAT number?

Click Update info, then Add a registered Tax ID or VAT number: pick the country, enter the number and click Save. According to Zapier's help, “it will automatically be applied to all past and future invoices in your Zapier billing history.”

I can't see my team's invoices.

“For Team and Enterprise plans, only account admins or owners can view or change billing information.”

Source: Zapier official help, billing information and tax information on invoices, read on 27/10/2026.

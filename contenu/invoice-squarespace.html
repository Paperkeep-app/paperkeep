Squarespace invoice: where to find and download it (exact path)

Squarespace does not mail or e-mail invoices, and does not tell you when a new one is ready: it waits in your account's Billing panel. Here is the path, according to Squarespace's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Open the Billing panel and click Invoices.
  2. Click an invoice to view its details.
  3. To print or download it, click the Print button near the bottom-right corner of the invoice.

Good to know: there is no PDF button. In the print window, choose “Save as PDF”. Invoices can only be printed or downloaded one at a time.

Source: Squarespace official help, viewing your account invoices, read on 27/10/2026.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I get the invoice by e-mail?

On purpose: “No. For security, we don't mail or email invoices, or notify you when you have a new invoice.” Collect them from the Billing panel.

Who can see the invoices?

“Owners, Administrators, and contributors with Billing permissions can access invoices.”

What about a domain invoice?

The help points to the Domains dashboard: pick the domain, then its billing and invoices.

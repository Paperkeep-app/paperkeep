Download your Zoom invoice: the exact path

Zoom doesn't always email you its invoice: it waits in your account. And if the provider isn't established in Belgium, it doesn't arrive through Peppol either. Here is where to find it, according to the provider's official help.

The path, step by step

  1. Sign in to the Zoom web portal.
  2. In the top-right corner, click your profile picture or initials, then Plans and billings.
  3. Open the Payment History or Invoice History tab, tick the invoice and click Download.

Good to know: only the account owner, admins and roles with billing permissions have access. Zoom is updating its web portal, so the path may look slightly different on your account.

Source: Zoom Support.
